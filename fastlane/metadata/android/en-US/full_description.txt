Reclaim your focus. ALauncher is a clean, distraction-free Android launcher built for people who want their phone to work for them, not against them.

ALauncher is 100% free and open-source forever with zero subscriptions. Runs completely offline with no ads, tracking, or accounts. Your notifications, app usage, and preferences never leave your device.

🔒 ACCESSIBILITY SERVICE USAGE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ALauncher includes an optional "Double-Tap to Lock" feature to turn off and lock your screen by double-tapping empty space on the home screen.

To provide this, ALauncher uses Android's AccessibilityService API:
• Purpose: Solely used to trigger the screen lock action (GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN).
• Preserves Biometrics: Acts just like pressing the physical power button, ensuring Fingerprint and Face Unlock continue to work normally.
• Privacy Guarantee: ALauncher does NOT collect, read, store, or transmit any personal data, keystrokes, screen content, or sensitive information.
• User Controlled: Entirely optional, disabled by default, and can be revoked at any time in Android Accessibility Settings.
