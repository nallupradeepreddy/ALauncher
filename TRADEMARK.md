## 3. Creating a Fork or Derivative Distribution
If you distribute a fork or derivative binary build of ALauncher to other users:
1. **Change the Package Name:** Modify `applicationId` in `app/build.gradle.kts` (e.g., change `com.alauncher.app` to `com.yourdomain.yourforkname`).
2. **Change the App Name:** Update `app_name` in `strings.xml` to your own distinct product name.
3. **Replace the App Icons:** Replace all `ic_launcher` drawables and mipmap assets with your own visual assets.
4. **Comply with GPLv3:** Distribute your complete modified source code under the GNU General Public License v3.0 as required by the license.
